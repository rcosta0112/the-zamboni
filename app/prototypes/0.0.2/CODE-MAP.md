# 0.0.2: code map

> **[Agent note]** Read this before opening source files; nothing else is needed to find code. All paths are in `src/`. Keep it to what each file owns: details belong in the file's header comment and in [`README.md`](README.md).

| File | Owns | Start at |
|---|---|---|
| `main.ts` | Start-up and load order; `?test` (dev: the checks' story, from `landed`); when the start button comes and what using it does; the animatic after NEW GAME (cutscene 1's card, then cutscene 2 watched); the fixed 60 Hz step; the frame loop (who is called, in what order); dev hooks on `window` | `start`, `begin` |
| `StartScreen.svelte` | The start screen: the mockup's layout, the bar then the buttons (START, or CONTINUE and NEW GAME when there's a save), which keys choose and use them | `press`, `move`, `setProgress`, `setReady` |
| `loading.ts` | What the start screen's bar measures: the load's parts (downloads, the shaders object by object, the warm-up frames) and their shares | `LoadProgress`, `downloads` |
| `settings.ts` | Every tunable value and its starting point | `settings` |
| `tuning.ts` | The tuning panel (lil-gui): one folder per area | `createTuningPanel` |
| `input.ts` | Actions from gamepad and keyboard: move, look, jump, interact, a menu's step (D-pad, stick), a cutscene's skip, last device used | `Input.poll`, `consume*`, `menuStep` |
| `character.ts` | Her movement (indoors: the way the stick points), jump (lower indoors) and gravity; flying with momentum (only while she wears a jetpack): hover and flight, the glide and the skid after; the auto-parachute (the jetpack slowing a long fall); placing her on a ladder; floating off a ladder's top | `Character.update`, `jet` (the jet on), `letGo`, `coast` (glide, skid), `moveBy`, `jump`, `floating`, `setClimbing`, `moveDirection` |
| `physics.ts` | Rapier: ground, static colliders (a part of a mesh can be its own, switchable), boxes, convex ones (the collision boxes, which rays pass through), her capsule and controller, moving colliders (doors), rays | `Physics.move`, `addStaticMesh`, `addStaticBox`, `addStaticConvex`, `addMovingConvex`, `ray`, `castRay`, `fits`, `overFloor` |
| `camera.ts` | Orbit camera: yaw, pitch, the fixed indoor and outdoor distances, follow, collision outside the ship | `FollowCamera.update` |
| `ship.ts` | Loading the ship, its collider and the collision boxes (the collider file's other meshes); "inside"; decks; see-through units and their tags; **the cargo ramp** (its closed and open angles); hinges; the merged shadow caster; the entities (and the ramp's `Openable`) | `loadShip`, `addCollisionBoxes`, `updateShip`, `closedAngle`, `groupCutUnits`, `hingeAt` |
| `entities.ts`, `kinds.ts` | The ship's things by id (`userData.id`), each with its kind and its components; the `Openable` component (open, amount, paused: what scenes, Ink and cutscenes will set); the kinds and their defaults (a door's movement, sounds, opening to the ground or by itself); `ofKind` finds the rest (the ramp, ladders, jetpacks, the boombox, the devices) | `Entities`, `Entity`, `DOOR_KINDS` |
| `scenes.ts`, `commands.ts`, `public/scenes/`, `public/test/scenes/` | Scenes: the data (location, her start, `travel`: the ship flying, the setup as commands); the game's scenes (`chapter-1`, `landed`) and the checks'; the director that starts one (the kinds' defaults, the flight rules: `FLIGHT_LOCKED`, `ship_flying`, the ship's noise; the setup, the doors snapped, her start); `?scene=<id>` in dev builds. | `story.ts`, `public/story/story.ink`, `public/test/story.ink` | The story (Ink): loading the compiled story (`npm run ink`), its variables, running a knot (its lines, with their speakers, to the dialogue box, up to its topics: Ink's choices; `choose` runs the one picked on), Ink's `command(name, id, value)` into the commands; the knots `use_<id>` (used while locked, or `# instead` of the action; its `# verb:` the prompt) and `talk_<id>`; the characters' names on screen | `GameStory`, `run`, `useKnot`, `talkKnot`, `displayName` |
| `dialogue.ts`, `packages/ui` `DialogueBox.svelte` | A conversation on screen: one line at a time, the typewriter and its key sound, complete / next / end, the topics (a screen of her lines in the box, the selection, the pick), the debounce and the cooldown after it | `Dialogue.start`, `update`, `onEnded`, `onChoose` |
| `cutscene.ts`, `public/test/cutscenes/` | Cutscenes: the track (shots of camera keys in the ship file's Blender coordinates, the far plane per shot, an event track of commands, the end state); the player with its own camera (the game's waits behind her: the hand-back), *skip* to the end state; main.ts holds her still, turns the see-through and the interactions off and shows the crew while one plays, and saves a checkpoint at its end (`onEnded`) | `CutscenePlayer.play`, `update`, `skip`, `Track` |

Outside `src/`: `index.html`; `src/style.css` has the prompt, help and pause overlays. The button, the progress bar and the tokens (colours, fonts) are in [`app/packages/ui`](../../packages/ui/README.md); the screen's images and fonts in `app/prototypes/0.0.2/public/ui/`. Scripts run outside the browser (exports, the scripted check `npm run smoke`, the one-question `npm run probe`, the records of changes to the ship file) are listed in [`app/prototypes/0.0.2/tools/README.md`](tools/README.md).

Until the start button is used, nothing is simulated: the frame stops after the sound while loading, then is made in full under the start screen. Per frame, in order (`main.ts`): input → sound (volumes, the ship's noise) → a cutscene (or skip) → fixed steps (ladders or character, then doors and ramp) → door and ramp sounds, the boombox, the jetpack rack, her outfit with Ink and her locker's clothes, the ladder's sound → her animation, footsteps → camera → the jetpack's glow → see-through rules → see-through shader values → interaction → world, crew, lights → render.
