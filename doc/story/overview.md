# The Zamboni

The Zamboni is a military transport Arbogast MM-41 converted into a research and exploration vessel. It’s set up for medium duration expeditions with capacity for a crew of 3 organics. Inorganic capacity would be dependent on the types of bodies employed. It’s standard practice to have two organic and one inorganic with one or two spare bodies.

## Summary

This is a road trip story

Adam is the robot character. Usually he's just a body (or two in this case).
They are on a long mission, so the two bodies slowly start to diverge. The ship is old and has no processing core. So even though they are more capable than humans in many aspects, their overall intelligence is on par. There’s only so much processing you can have on a body.

The story is about how the crew reacts to Adam's change in personality. But it's defo not "kill all humans". It's more them being distracted by things and being unreliable and emotionally volatile

They also feel lonely?

So I guess the vibe is a bit like going on a road trip with a friend that is going through some stuff. Maybe a bad break up? How do you make a construct feel better?

## The mission

When the portal leading to BMTM-640 B was found, a survey team was sent in to locate and catalog any alien artifacts, structures and life forms.

Their primary objective is to investigate potential portals. Every planet on the portal network has proved to have at least one additional portal to another location. Some have several. The ship is equipped with a sensor array that can detect their signature, but the signals are faint and erratic. Dr. Ogawa is responsible for operating and maintaining the equipment and the ship.
