### Dr. Ogawa and Dr. Green talk about Adam being weird lately

Dr. Ogawa and Dr Green have a conversation about how Adam is behaving more and more strangely lately. Dr. Ogawa explains that due to his isolation from Mainframe his personality is taking over. Main uses personalities as protocols to better interact with humans and will use different ones with different people and situations.

**Green**\
Do you think they’re ok?

**Ogawa**\
As far as I can tell.\
I’ve heard this can happen to remotes on long missions.

**Green**\
Because they can’t sync up with Main?

**Ogawa**\
Yes. Being off the grid is good for most humans, but for Adam it means being disconnected from the bigger part of their own mind.

**Green**\
That sounds rough. Do you think they are depressed?

**Ogawa**\
I think it’s more like they are diverging. They are becoming a separate entity.\
It’s hard to predict where that would lead to.

**Green**\
What should we do?

**Ogawa**\
I don’t know.\
Be nice to them?\
We ran some diagnostics and there’s no mechanical issues other than a couple worn servos.
