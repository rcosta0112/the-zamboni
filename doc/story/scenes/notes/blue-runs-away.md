### Blue runs away

**Adam**\
Dr. Green, something is wrong.

**Green**\
What happened?

**Adam**\
They are gone.

**Green**\
Gone? What do you mean?

**Adam**\
Unit 2 broke connection.\
I can’t…\
I can’t reach them.

**Green**\
Maybe there’s a malfunction? Where are they now?

**Adam**\
I don’t know. We were close to the ship.

**Green**\
Dr. Ogawa, is… Adam there?

**Ogawa**\
I thought they were with you? At least one of them?\
I’ve been in the lab for the past hour. Didn’t notice when they left.

**Green**\
Adam lost contact with Unit 2.

**Ogawa**\
Can’t hail them on the comms. It’s the same channel for both units.\
Let me check the sensors.

**Green**\
Adam, did Unit 2 say anything?

**Adam**\
No. I had this feeling.\
They just… disconnected.

**Ogawa**\
I can see a blip about a kilometer to the North.

**Green**\
Let’s go. Dr. Ogawa, please fire up the engines. We’re coming back.
